What happens when freedom becomes a language for looking away from pain?

This essay asks where personal choice ends and collective responsibility begins. It looks at the stories a market tells about dignity, progress, and consent. It also asks what is lost when every part of life can be priced.

> Freedom should create more dignity and possibility. It should not make suffering easier to ignore.
