## The observation

Ideas are more useful when you can see how they connect. A score alone does not explain why a result matters.

## The response

Creative Graph keeps the path visible. It exposes direction, ranking signals, and uncertainty so a person can judge the result.

## Where it is now

The retrieval system is working, but the product story is still developing. It remains a draft on this portfolio until that story is ready.
